Fundraising & Investor Readiness

The financial model, investor narrative, and data room — built to withstand institutional investor diligence.

Most founders arrive at a fundraise with a product story and a spreadsheet. Investors have seen thousands of rounds. The gap between a fundable business and a fundable financial story is where most processes stall.

What the engagement covers
Financial ModelDriver-based 3-statement model with cohort analysis, unit economics, and scenario outputs — investor-grade from the first meeting.
Investor NarrativeThe financial story that matches the product story — so the numbers support the pitch, not contradict it.
Data Room PreparationStructure, content, and diligence readiness — built to answer questions before they're asked.
Diligence SupportDirect support through investor diligence — the same team that built the materials, available to answer the questions.
