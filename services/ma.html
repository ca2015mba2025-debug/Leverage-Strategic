Supporting Capability — Transactions

Transaction Advisory

Financial, commercial, and structural transaction support — for founders buying or selling, where the wrong structure costs more than the advisory fee.

Most founders only do one or two significant transactions in their lifetime. The asymmetry between founder and advisor experience is where founders get hurt — on deal structure, earn-out mechanics, working capital pegs, and positions they didn't know were negotiable.

What the engagement covers
LOI & Deal ReviewReview of deal structure proposals — earn-outs, working capital pegs, deferred consideration — before you sign.
Sell-Side PositioningFinancials, narrative, and information memorandum — so buyers see the strongest version of what you've built.
Buy-Side SupportCommercial and financial assessment of target businesses — so you enter negotiation with a clear view of value and risk.
Negotiation SupportA transaction advisor in the room — someone who knows what's standard, what's aggressive, and what to push back on.
Deal StructuringAsset vs. share, earn-out design, and post-close mechanics — before you're committed to a position.
