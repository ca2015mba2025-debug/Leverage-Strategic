Finance Leadership & FP&A

Senior finance leadership without the full-time cost — the strategic layer that lets you run the business like a CFO is in the room.

There is a moment in every growth-stage business when the founder realises the finance function is broken. The numbers aren't reliable. The board is asking questions that take a week to answer. This is the moment senior finance leadership pays for itself.

What the engagement covers
Board & Investor ReportingBoard pack preparation, KPI dashboards, and investor update materials — built to build confidence, not generate questions.
Financial Planning & AnalysisRolling forecasts, scenario models, and budget-versus-actuals review.
Financial ModellingDriver-based 3-statement models, integrated and scenario-ready — built to be used in board meetings, not archived.
Cash Flow GovernanceCash runway modelling, working capital management, and early warning signals.
Fundraise PreparationData room structure, financial model, investor narrative, and diligence readiness — built before the process begins.
Unit Economics & MarginRevenue streams, cost drivers, contribution margins, and pricing decisions.
Finance Function Build-OutOversight of existing staff, hiring support, and finance team structure.
