Strategic Finance & Decision Support

Financial analysis for the decisions that matter — grounded in the numbers, not a slide deck disconnected from them.

Every growth, pricing, or business-model decision has a financial answer buried inside it. This capability connects the decision in front of you to what it actually costs, what it returns, and what it requires financially to execute.

What the engagement covers
Strategic Financial AnalysisFinancially material growth, pricing, and business-model decisions assessed against the numbers.
Scenario ModellingBase, upside, and downside scenarios — so you can stress-test a decision before you're committed to it.
Unit EconomicsCAC, LTV, payback period, and contribution margin built to withstand board and investor scrutiny.
Profitability AnalysisMargin diagnostics and a prioritized plan to improve unit economics and cash generation.
Capital AllocationWhere the next dollar of capital should go, and what the return looks like.
Forecasting & Decision SupportRolling forecasts and financial decision frameworks that finance can actually track.
