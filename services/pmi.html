Post-Acquisition Integration

The deal doesn't end at close. Integration support so the value underwritten in diligence is the value actually captured.

Most acquisition support ends the day the deal closes — right when the real work of capturing value begins. This capability extends the relationship past close: 100-day planning, financial integration, and the reporting and KPI framework a newly acquired business needs to run well.

What the engagement covers
100-Day PlanningA prioritized integration plan covering finance, reporting, and the operating changes that matter first.
Financial IntegrationChart of accounts, reporting cadence, and financial systems brought in line with how the new owner needs to run the business.
KPI & Management ReportingThe KPI framework and board/investor reporting the business needs from day one of ownership.
Working Capital OptimizationPost-close review of working capital, cash conversion, and early operational wins.
Finance Function Build-OutStanding up or strengthening the finance function inside the acquired business.
