FAQ

Common questions.

What does Leverage Strategic do?

M&A support, fundraising materials, due diligence and QoE, financial models, fractional CFO and FP&A, and bookkeeping. One team, whichever you need.

What size companies do you work with?

Growth-stage and mid-market businesses, acquisition entrepreneurs, investors and advisory firms. Fractional CFO clients are typically $2M-$20M in revenue. Diligence and transaction work spans $1M-$100M deals.

Which engagement is best for me?

Start from the situation. Buying a business points to diligence and deal support. Raising capital points to the model, materials and data room. Selling points to sell-side readiness. Finance that has stopped keeping pace points to a fractional CFO. Unsure? A short call will sort it out.

How quickly can we start?

After the intro call, scope and timing are confirmed in a proposal. For urgent deals we can compress the timeline.

Is bookkeeping your main service?

No. Our core is deal, capital and strategic finance work. We also do bookkeeping and reporting, so the numbers behind your deal or raise are clean.

Where do you work?

Canada, the US and the UK, including cross-border deals. Onsite, hybrid or remote, depending on the work.

How long does diligence take?

A standard QoE typically takes three to five weeks. We can compress that for time-sensitive deals.

What does it cost?

Scoped per engagement. Fractional CFO and bookkeeping run as monthly retainers, projects are priced by scope. Typically 30-50% below a mid-market firm.

Can you work behind our firm?

Yes. We support banks and advisory firms with white-label delivery, and work alongside your lawyers, lenders and accountants.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. We are happy to sign an NDA before you share anything sensitive.
