Privacy

How Leverage Strategic collects and uses information on this website. Last updated November 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Leverage Strategic, a Leverage 10X company. Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@leveragestrategic.com.

Information you give us

When you send a message or request a report, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, company and message. We use them to respond to you and to follow up about the report or relevant advisory services. These submissions are delivered to us by email through our form provider, formsubmit.co.

Anonymous analytics (only with your consent)

If you accept, we collect anonymous information about how the site is used: pages viewed, links and buttons clicked, files downloaded, how far you scroll, time spent, the sections you spend time in, approximate cursor activity, the website or campaign that referred you, your country and city (derived from your IP address), and your device type. We do not store your IP address. It is converted into a daily-changing anonymous code. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell this information.

Local storage

To recognise a returning browser and to link the pages of a single visit, the site stores a few small items in your browser (lv_vid, lv_sid, lv_utm_*, and lv_consent for your choice). They contain no personal details. If you decline, or if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, none of the analytics items are created.

Service providers

  • Hosting and server functions: Vercel.
  • Analytics storage: Supabase.
  • Form delivery: formsubmit.co.
  • Search performance data: Google Search Console, which gives us aggregated data about searches that led to this site.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw consent. Write to hello@leveragestrategic.com and we will respond.
