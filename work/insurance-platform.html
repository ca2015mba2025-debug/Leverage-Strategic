Insurance Distribution Platform

Insurance Distribution / InsurTech

Situation

Technology-enabled insurance distribution platform generating $85M revenue and $12M EBITDA (14% margin), with 28% revenue growth and 60% gross margin. Operates a network of 18,000+ independent agents, 1.8M+ policies placed and 40+ carrier partners.

Mandate

Investment Committee evaluation of an acquisition at an implied $150M enterprise value (12.5x EBITDA).

Scope of Analysis

Market Structure & Fragmentation AnalysisAgent EconomicsEmbedded SaaS OpportunityValuationAcquisition FinancingRisk AssessmentReturn Scenarios

Selected Findings

The thesis centers on distribution efficiency, consolidation and technology monetization rather than pure market growth — commissions plus a prospective high-margin SaaS revenue stream from underwriting and claims technology.

Transaction Perspective

Illustrative case developed to demonstrate Investment Committee-level evaluation of a platform-consolidation thesis in a fragmented distribution market.
