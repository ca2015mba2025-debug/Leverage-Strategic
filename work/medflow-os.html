MedFlow OS

Healthcare Vertical SaaS

Metric$11.3M ARR

Situation

Cloud EHR, scheduling, billing and revenue-cycle-management platform serving 84 clinic groups (~310 locations, 22 specialties, 67 employees). $12.0M LTM revenue, $11.3M ARR, $2.4M EBITDA (20% margin), 108% NRR, 91% GRR and 5.8-year average customer tenure.

Mandate

Full acquisition underwriting for a prospective buyer at a proposed $14.4M purchase price (6.0x EBITDA), evaluated against a 13.9x comparable-transaction median.

Scope of Analysis

Investment ThesisManagement AssessmentSaaS KPI & Cohort AnalysisQuality of EarningsSources & UsesComparable TransactionsReturns & Sensitivity AnalysisTAM/SAM/SOM100-Day PlanValue Creation

Selected Findings

Investment case anchored on a mission-critical clinical workflow, high switching costs, recurring revenue and founder-succession-driven deal availability, with a path to expand EBITDA margin from 20% toward ~29%. Modeled an AI documentation add-on worth ~$3.1M incremental ARR at ~80% gross margin.

Transaction Perspective

Recommendation: Pursue. 43% modeled gross IRR and 6.0x MoM under the base case.
