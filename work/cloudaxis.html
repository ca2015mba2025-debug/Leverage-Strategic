Situation

Workflow automation SaaS serving 340+ professional-services firms across legal, accounting, staffing and consulting. 94% GRR, 107% NRR, 92% recurring revenue, 4.8-year customer tenure, ~23% Adj. EBITDA margin and 90%+ cash conversion.

Mandate

Evaluated an off-market acquisition opportunity created by founder succession, at a $10.0M enterprise value (3.6x EBITDA / 0.83x ARR, up to $11M including earnout).

Scope of Analysis

Investment ThesisSaaS Metrics & Cohort AnalysisQuality of EarningsValuationDeal StructuringReturns Modeling100-Day Plan

Selected Findings

Identified untapped pricing (unchanged since 2021), the absence of an outbound sales motion or dedicated CSM function, and 20+ fragmented bolt-on targets as the principal value-creation levers. Flagged customer concentration risk, with the top 25 accounts representing 47% of ARR.

Transaction Perspective

Attractive valuation arbitrage supported by recurring-revenue quality, tempered by concentration risk requiring transaction protection.
