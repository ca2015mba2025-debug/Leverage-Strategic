How is financial due diligence priced?

What drives the cost of financial due diligence, and how to keep it sensible.

There is no single price for financial due diligence. The fee follows the scope of the work, and a clear scope keeps the cost under control.

What drives the cost

Deal size, the number of entities, the quality of the books, how many years are reviewed, and the deadline. Messy records and tight timelines add effort.

How to keep it sensible

Agree the scope first. Share clean data early. Ask the adviser to focus on the areas that move price: earnings quality, working capital and net debt.

Our approach

We scope each engagement up front. Our delivery model typically costs 30 to 50 percent less than a mid-market firm, with partner-level review on every engagement.
