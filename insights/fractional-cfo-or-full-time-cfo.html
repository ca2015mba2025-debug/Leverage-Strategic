Fractional CFO or full-time CFO?

When part-time finance leadership is enough, and when a full-time hire makes sense.

Many growing businesses need CFO-level thinking long before they need a full-time CFO.

When a fractional CFO fits

You need forecasts, cash-flow control, board reporting or fundraising support, but not five days a week. You want senior judgement without a large salary.

When to hire full-time

Finance has become a daily, company-wide function, with a team to lead and constant decisions to make.

A practical path

Start with a fractional CFO on a monthly retainer. Build the reporting and the forecast. Hire full-time when the workload clearly asks for it.
