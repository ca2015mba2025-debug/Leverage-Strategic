What is a Quality of Earnings (QoE) report?

A QoE report tests whether a company's reported profit is real, repeatable and ready to be paid for.

A Quality of Earnings (QoE) report is an independent review of a company's profit. It asks one question: is the EBITDA on the page real, and will it repeat?

What a QoE looks at

Reported earnings are adjusted for one-off items, owner expenses and accounting choices. The review also checks revenue quality, customer concentration, margins, working capital and net debt.

Why buyers and sellers use it

Buyers use a QoE to avoid paying for profit that is not there. Sellers use one before a sale, so surprises do not appear late and reduce the price.

How long it takes

A standard QoE typically takes three to five weeks, depending on the quality of the records. Timelines can be compressed for urgent deals.
